The [[ ]] construct is a bash extension that provides more features than the standard [ ] (test command). Key differences:

[[ ]] supports pattern matching with == and =~
[[ ]] doesn't require quoting variables as often
[[ ]] has more intuitive handling of logical operators (&& and || instead of -a and -o)
[[ ]] is generally safer and more powerful
